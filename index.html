About

I build machine learning tools for physiological signals and the brain, from cardiac rhythms measured through a camera to the neural circuits of emotion.

I am an M.S. student in Biomedical Engineering at Boston University, supported by the Dean’s Graduate Scholarship. As a computer engineering undergraduate at Daejeon University, I led research on estimating heart rate variability from remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) for atrial fibrillation detection. At the Korea Brain Research Institute, I combined deep learning–based facial expression analysis with immunohistochemistry, cryostat sectioning, and patch-clamp electrophysiology to study emotion-related circuits in the mouse brain.
