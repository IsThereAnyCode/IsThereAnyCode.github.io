About

I am a first-year M.S. student in Biomedical Engineering at Boston University and a research assistant in Frank Guenther’s Speech Neuroscience Lab, which studies how the brain controls speech. Previously, I gained hands-on wet-lab experience at the Korea Brain Research Institute, from immunohistochemistry to patch-clamp electrophysiology, studying emotion-related circuits in the mouse brain.

Outside the lab, I enjoy Kaggle competitions, soccer, and basketball.
